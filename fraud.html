🛡️ Fraud Alerts & Safety Tips

Know the common scams so you can protect your money.

🚨 Golden rule

Never share your OTP, UPI PIN, card PIN, CVV or passwords with anyone. Not even someone who says they are from your bank, from the police, or from a government office.

Stay safe checklist

  • Download banking and UPI apps only from the Play Store / App Store.
  • Set a screen lock on your phone and keep your UPI PIN secret.
  • Check the name shown before you confirm any UPI payment.
  • Do not click links in unknown SMS or WhatsApp messages.
  • Check your bank SMS alerts regularly for transactions you did not make.
  • If you are cheated, call the cyber crime helpline 1930 immediately and report at cybercrime.gov.in.
